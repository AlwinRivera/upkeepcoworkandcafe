About Upkeep

Your work and study buddy in Northern Cebu

Upkeep started as a spot to get out of the house and actually get something done — now it's where students prep for the bar, freelancers take client calls, and small teams meet, all under the same planter walls. We keep the essentials right: fast internet, comfortable seating, quiet corners, and coffee worth ordering twice.

A little magic goes a long way.
